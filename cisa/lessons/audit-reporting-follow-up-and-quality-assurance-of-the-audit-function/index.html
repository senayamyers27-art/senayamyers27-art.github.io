ISACA Certified Information Systems Auditor (CISA) 2024 job practice · Domain 1: Information system auditing process

Audit reporting, follow-up and quality assurance of the audit function

▶ Watch the overview video

Last reviewed September 29, 2026 · Leer en español

An audit only changes things when its results are communicated clearly and acted on. Reporting, follow-up and quality assurance close the loop and keep the audit function credible.

A well-written finding has five parts: condition (what the auditor found), criteria (what should be, such as a policy or standard), cause (why the gap exists), effect (the risk or impact) and recommendation. Findings are discussed with the auditee before the report is final, usually at an exit meeting, to confirm facts and obtain management responses that name corrective actions, owners and dates. If management disagrees, the auditor rechecks the evidence; if the finding still stands, it stays in the report along with management's view. The report states scope, objectives, period covered, an overall conclusion and any scope limitations, and it is distributed to the audit committee and appropriate management.

Follow-up verifies that agreed actions were actually implemented and effective. The auditor tracks open findings and tests the fixes; management's statement that something is done is not enough. If management chooses to accept a risk the auditor considers unacceptable, the auditor discusses it with senior management and, if unresolved, the audit committee. The auditor never implements the fix, because that would impair independence.

Quality assurance and improvement cover the audit function itself. They include supervision and review of workpapers, ongoing internal monitoring, periodic self-assessments and external quality assessments against standards, and feedback from auditees. Metrics such as plan completion, time to report and percentage of recommendations implemented help the function improve.

Key terms

Condition
The part of a finding that states what the auditor actually observed.
Criteria
The standard, policy or expectation that the condition is measured against.
Exit meeting
A meeting at the end of fieldwork where findings are discussed with management before the report is finalized.
Follow-up
Audit procedures that verify whether agreed corrective actions were implemented and effective.
Real-world example

An audit finds that firewall changes are not reviewed. Management agrees to introduce monthly reviews by the network manager by March. In April the auditor samples three monthly reviews, confirms they were performed and documented, and closes the finding.

Exam tip: Auditors report and verify; they do not fix. Any option where the auditor implements the corrective action or quietly drops a supported finding is wrong.

Check yourself

What should an auditor do if management disagrees with a finding?

Re-examine the evidence; if the finding is still supported, report it along with management's response.

Name two activities in an audit quality assurance program.

Any two of: workpaper supervision and review, internal self-assessments, external quality assessments, auditee feedback surveys and performance metrics.
