Reading printed and handwritten text with the OCR (Read) feature of Azure AI Vision

▶ Watch the overview video

Last reviewed September 25, 2026 · Leer en español

Optical character recognition (OCR) turns text in images into machine-readable text. Azure AI Vision provides it through the Read feature, which recognizes printed and handwritten text in many languages in photos, screenshots and scans: street signs, product labels, whiteboards, posters and handwritten notes.

In Image Analysis 4.0, Read is simply one of the visual features: you request read along with, or instead of, caption and tags, and the call returns synchronously. The result is organized into blocks, lines and words. Each line has its text and a bounding polygon (four corner points, so rotated text is described accurately), and each word has its text, polygon and a confidence score. Your code can join lines into a transcript, search for specific words or use positions to understand layout, such as which text appears near a logo.

result = client.analyze(image_data=data, visual_features=[VisualFeatures.READ])
for block in result.read.blocks:
    for line in block.lines:
        print(line.text)
        for word in line.words:
            if word.confidence < 0.8:
                print('  low confidence:', word.text)

Choose the right OCR service for the job. Vision Read is optimized for general, non-document images: scenes, signs, labels and short text. For documents such as multi-page PDFs, forms, invoices and scanned reports, Azure AI Document Intelligence is the better choice: its read model handles large, multi-page documents, and its layout and prebuilt models add paragraphs, tables, selection marks and named fields. The earlier asynchronous Read API in Computer Vision 3.2 (submit, get an Operation-Location, poll for results) is what Document Intelligence's read model grew from, and you may still see it in older code. OCR is also available as a built-in skill in Azure AI Search, so scanned files can be made searchable during indexing.

Quality depends on the input. Use good resolution and lighting, avoid heavy blur and extreme angles, and check word confidence scores. Handwriting recognition works well for clear writing but is less reliable for messy script, so plan for human review of low-confidence words. If you need only numbers or specific fields from a known form, a Document Intelligence model gives structured output instead of raw text you must parse.

Key terms

OCR
Optical character recognition: extracting machine-readable text from images of text.
Read feature
The Azure AI Vision capability that extracts printed and handwritten text with positions and confidence.
Bounding polygon
The corner points outlining where a line or word appears in the image, including rotated text.
Line and word
The levels at which Read returns text, each with position; words also include confidence scores.
Real-world example

A logistics company photographs shipping labels with handheld scanners. Vision Read extracts the lines of text, the app finds the tracking number pattern in them and flags words under 0.8 confidence for a worker to confirm, while full delivery paperwork goes to Document Intelligence instead.

Exam tip: Text in photos and scenes points to Vision Read; long documents, tables and form fields point to Document Intelligence. Read returns lines and words with polygons and word confidence.

Check yourself

What structure does Read return?

Blocks containing lines, each with text and a bounding polygon, and words with text, polygon and a confidence score.

Why use Document Intelligence rather than Vision Read for a 40-page scanned contract?

Document Intelligence is built for multi-page documents and adds structure such as paragraphs and tables, while Vision Read is optimized for general images.
