Cloud Billing accounts: linking projects to billing and the billing IAM roles

▶ Watch the overview video

Last reviewed September 25, 2026

A Cloud Billing account defines who pays for Google Cloud usage. It holds the payment profile (a credit card or invoiced billing) and is linked to one or more projects. A project can be linked to only one billing account at a time, but one billing account can pay for many projects. If a project has no active billing account, its paid resources stop: VMs are shut down and billable services become unavailable, and eventually data can be deleted.

Billing accounts are usually owned by the organization, and they have their own IAM roles, separate from project roles. Billing Account Administrator manages everything about the account, including payment methods, users and linking. Billing Account User lets someone link projects to that billing account; combined with a project role such as Project Billing Manager or Owner on the project, it allows creating the link. Billing Account Viewer can see costs and transactions without changing anything. Billing Account Costs Manager can manage budgets and view and export cost information. There is also Project Billing Manager, granted on a project or higher, which lets someone attach or detach that project's billing but not see the billing account's details.

You link or change billing in the console (Billing > Account management, or the project's Billing page) or with the CLI: gcloud billing projects link PROJECT_ID --billing-account=0X0X0X-0X0X0X-0X0X0X. gcloud billing accounts list shows the accounts you can see.

Some companies use several billing accounts, for example one per business unit or per reseller agreement, but most use one and split costs with labels, projects and reports. Moving a project to a different billing account is a relinking operation and doesn't move or change resources.

A common least-privilege pattern: the finance team has Billing Account Administrator; a platform team that creates projects has Billing Account User so they can attach new projects; and developers have no billing roles at all.

Key terms

Cloud Billing account
The account that pays for usage in the projects linked to it.
Billing Account User
A role that allows linking projects to a billing account without managing it.
Billing Account Administrator
A role that manages a billing account's payment settings, users and links.
Project Billing Manager
A project-level role that allows attaching or detaching the project's billing.
Real-world example

A platform team automates new project creation with Terraform. Its service account has Project Creator on a folder and Billing Account User on the corporate billing account, so it can create a project and link it to billing, but it can't view invoices or change the payment method.

Exam tip: 'Link projects but not change the billing account' is Billing Account User. 'View costs only' is Billing Account Viewer. 'Manage everything' is Billing Account Administrator.

Check yourself

Can a project be linked to two billing accounts?

No. A project has at most one billing account at a time, though one billing account can pay for many projects.

What happens to a project's paid resources if billing is disabled?

They stop working; for example VMs are shut down, and data may eventually be deleted.
