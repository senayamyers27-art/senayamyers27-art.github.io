Azure AI Fundamentals vs Azure AI Engineer: which should you take?

AI-900 is the introduction to AI concepts and Azure AI services; AI-102 is for engineers who build AI solutions on Azure.

Which first?

Take Azure AI Fundamentals first. AI-900 gives the vocabulary and service overview that AI-102 assumes, though it isn't required.

Who each one is for

Azure AI Fundamentals (AI-900): Beginners and non-developers who want to understand AI workloads, machine learning basics and responsible AI.

Azure AI Engineer (AI-102): Developers and engineers who build solutions with Azure AI services, search and generative AI.

Key differences

  • AI-102 expects you to work with SDKs, REST APIs and deployment; AI-900 is about knowing what each service does.
  • AI-102 covers designing, securing and monitoring solutions; AI-900 stays at the concept level.
  • Both cover responsible AI principles.
