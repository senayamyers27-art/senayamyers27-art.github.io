Azure Fundamentals vs Cloud Digital Leader: which should you take?

Both are foundational cloud certifications: AZ-900 for Microsoft Azure, Cloud Digital Leader for Google Cloud.

Which first?

Either order works. Choose the cloud your employer uses; the concepts transfer between them.

Who each one is for

Azure Fundamentals (AZ-900): Beginners at organizations that use Azure or Microsoft 365.

Cloud Digital Leader (Cloud Digital Leader): Beginners and business-focused staff at organizations that use Google Cloud, or who work with data and AI on Google.

Key differences

  • AZ-900 includes more specific Azure services and management tools; Cloud Digital Leader leans toward business value, digital transformation and data.
  • Cloud Digital Leader is often taken by non-technical staff; AZ-900 is also a common first step for Azure administrators.
  • The natural next steps are AZ-104 for Azure and Associate Cloud Engineer for Google Cloud.
