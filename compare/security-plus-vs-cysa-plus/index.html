Security+ vs CySA+: which should you take?

Security+ is the broad entry-level security certification; CySA+ is the next step for people doing analyst work: logs, alerts, vulnerability management and incident response.

Which first?

Take Security+ first. CySA+ assumes the concepts Security+ covers, and CompTIA recommends Security+ (or equivalent knowledge) first.

Who each one is for

Security+ (SY0-701): Anyone starting in security, or IT staff who need a recognized security baseline for a job or a government role.

CySA+ (CS0-004): People working, or about to work, in a SOC or security analyst role who want to prove hands-on analysis skills.

Key differences

  • Security+ covers every area of security at a basic level; CySA+ goes deep on detection, analysis and response.
  • CySA+ questions expect you to read log excerpts, scan results and command output and decide what to do.
  • Many employers use Security+ as a hiring filter; CySA+ shows you can do the analyst job itself.
