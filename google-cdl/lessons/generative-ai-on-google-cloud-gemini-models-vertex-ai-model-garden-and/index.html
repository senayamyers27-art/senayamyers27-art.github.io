Generative AI on Google Cloud: Gemini models, Vertex AI Model Garden and Vertex AI Studio

▶ Watch the overview video

Last reviewed September 25, 2026 · Leer en español

Generative AI lets organizations create text, images, code and more from natural language prompts. Google Cloud provides access to Google's own foundation models and to many others, along with tools to test, customize and deploy them securely for business use.

Gemini is Google's family of multimodal foundation models. Multimodal means they can understand and work with several types of input, such as text, images, audio, video and code, in the same request. Businesses use them to summarize documents, answer questions, draft content, extract information, write and explain code, and power chat assistants. Different model sizes trade capability against speed and cost. Google also offers specialized models, for example for image generation, and model names and versions change often, so focus on capabilities rather than version numbers.

Vertex AI Model Garden is a catalog where you can discover, compare and deploy models: Google's first-party models, models from partners, and open models. A team can evaluate several options for a task and choose the best balance of quality, cost and control, including open models it can host itself.

Vertex AI Studio is a console environment for prototyping with generative models. You can write and refine prompts, adjust settings, compare responses, and then get code to call the model from an application. From there, models can be customized: prompt design, grounding in your own data, or tuning with examples.

Enterprises need more than a clever model. Vertex AI provides security controls, data governance and responsible AI features such as safety filters. Google Cloud states that customer prompts and data used with its enterprise generative AI services are not used to train Google's models without permission. Gemini is also built into other Google products, such as Google Workspace for productivity and Gemini for Google Cloud to help people build and operate on the platform.

Prompt quality matters: clear instructions, context, a role, the desired format and examples usually produce better results. This is called prompt engineering.

Key terms

Gemini
Google's family of multimodal foundation models.
Multimodal
Able to process more than one type of data, such as text, images and audio, together.
Vertex AI Model Garden
A catalog in Vertex AI for discovering, testing and deploying Google, partner and open models.
Prompt engineering
Designing inputs to a generative model to get more useful and reliable outputs.
Real-world example

An insurance company uses Vertex AI Studio to test prompts that summarize long claim files into a one-page brief. After comparing two models from Model Garden on quality and cost, it deploys the chosen Gemini model behind an internal app, with safety filters and access controls in place.

Exam tip: Model Garden is for finding and choosing models; Vertex AI Studio is for testing prompts and prototyping; Gemini is the model family. Multimodal means text plus images, audio or video in the same model.
