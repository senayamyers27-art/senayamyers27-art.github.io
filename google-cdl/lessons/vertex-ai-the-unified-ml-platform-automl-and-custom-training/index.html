Vertex AI: the unified ML platform, AutoML and custom training

▶ Watch the overview video

Last reviewed September 25, 2026 · Leer en español

Vertex AI is Google Cloud's unified platform for building, deploying and managing machine learning and generative AI. Before unified platforms, teams stitched together separate tools for preparing data, training, tracking experiments, serving models and monitoring them. Vertex AI brings these steps together, so data scientists and engineers work in one place with consistent security and governance.

Vertex AI offers two main ways to build your own predictive models. AutoML lets you train a high-quality model on your own labeled data with little or no code. You provide the data, for example images labeled 'defect' or 'no defect', or a table with a target column, choose the objective, and AutoML handles model selection and tuning. It suits teams with domain data but limited ML expertise, and it supports tabular, image, text and video data.

Custom training gives full control. Data scientists write their own training code using frameworks such as TensorFlow, PyTorch or scikit-learn, choose the machine types, GPUs or TPUs, and tune every detail. It is the right choice when the problem is unique, when the model is a source of competitive advantage, or when AutoML cannot meet the requirements. It needs more skill and time.

Around training, Vertex AI provides the tools needed to run ML in production, often called MLOps: managed datasets and labeling, Workbench notebooks, pipelines to automate workflows, a model registry to track versions, endpoints to serve online predictions and batch prediction for large jobs, feature management, model evaluation and explanations, and model monitoring to detect drift.

Vertex AI is also the home of Google Cloud's generative AI tools, including access to Gemini and other foundation models, Model Garden and Vertex AI Studio, covered in a later lesson. For the exam, remember: AutoML for custom models with little expertise, custom training for full control, and Vertex AI as the platform that unifies the ML lifecycle.

Key terms

Vertex AI
Google Cloud's unified platform for building, deploying and managing ML and generative AI.
AutoML
Training a custom model on your own data with little or no code; the service handles model design and tuning.
Custom training
Training with your own code and framework for full control over the model.
MLOps
Practices and tools for deploying, monitoring and maintaining ML models in production.
Real-world example

A furniture maker has 20,000 photos of chair joints labeled good or faulty but no ML team. It uses AutoML image training in Vertex AI, deploys the model to an endpoint, and the factory camera system calls it for every chair. Later, a hired data scientist uses custom training to build a more specialized model.

Exam tip: Own labeled data plus little ML expertise points to AutoML. Unique, differentiating problem plus skilled data scientists points to custom training. Both run on Vertex AI.

Check yourself

When is AutoML a better fit than custom training?

When a team has its own labeled data but limited ML expertise and needs a good custom model quickly.

Name three MLOps capabilities in Vertex AI.

Any three of: pipelines, model registry, endpoints for serving, batch prediction, model monitoring, evaluation and explanations, feature management.
